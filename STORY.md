# Pip's Big Picture: the story behind the 10 levels

> *"Every friend is a piece of the big picture."* (Pip)

Diwali is coming, and Jalebi Junction is going to have its first **Friendship Mela**. Pip, the puzzle piece,
has a big idea: invite a friend from every corner of India. Gappu packs a basket of jalebis, and everyone
climbs aboard the **Jalebi Express**.

The train goes all the way round India. At every stop the friends meet someone new, join in with their
festival, try their food and solve their puzzles. When a level is done, the new friend hops on the train
and brings a gift for the Mela. In the last level the train gets home on Diwali night, the Mela begins,
and the last jigsaw is **The Big Picture**: every friend together, under the lights.

The route goes north → west → south → east → north-east → home, so a child who plays it all has "travelled"
the whole country.

```
 1 Jalebi Junction ─► 2 Punjab ─► 3 Rajasthan ─► 4 Gujarat ─► 5 Mumbai
                                                                   │
 10 Home for Diwali ◄─ 9 The Hills ◄─ 8 Kolkata ◄─ 7 Tamil Nadu ◄─ 6 Kerala
```

## The friends

The six friends from Jalebi Junction ride the whole way: **Gappu** the goat (runs the jalebi shop),
**Mithu** the parrot, **Tillu** the tortoise, **Chunnu** the cheeky monkey (he's the monkey in every maze),
**Gauri** the cow, and **Pip**. Eight new friends join them, one per stop:

| Level | Friend | From | What makes them *them* | Gift for the Mela |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Bholu** the water buffalo | Punjab | Gentle and strong; plays the dhol at Baisakhi; orange phulkari scarf | His dhol (drum) |
| 3 | **Kesar** the camel | Rajasthan | Long lashes, pom-pom headband, mirror-work saddle cloth; loves puppet shows | Kathputli puppets |
| 4 | **Raja** the Asiatic lion | Gujarat (Gir forest) | Big friendly mane; never misses Uttarayan, the kite festival | Kites |
| 5 | **Chikki** the mouse | Mumbai | Tiny and quick, in a yellow monsoon raincoat; makes modaks at Ganesh Chaturthi | Modaks |
| 6 | **Appu** the elephant | Kerala | Golden nettipattam on his forehead; cheers at the snake-boat race | Flowers for the pookalam |
| 7 | **Mayil** the peacock | Tamil Nadu | Shining blue, a fan of eyes; draws a kolam every morning; Pongal | Sugarcane |
| 8 | **Bagha** the tiger | West Bengal (Sundarbans) | Shy and kind; red-and-white Durga Puja scarf; sweet tooth | Rasgullas |
| 9 | **Momo** the red panda | Sikkim | Fluffy striped tail, white mask; rides the toy train; loves momos | Momos |

Each friend's state animal or best-known animal: the camel is Rajasthan's state animal, Gir is the only wild
home of Asiatic lions, the elephant is Kerala's state animal, the peacock is India's national bird, the
Bengal tiger is the national animal, and the red panda is Sikkim's state animal. The mouse is the friend of
Ganesha, whose festival Mumbai is famous for. *Mayil* is Tamil for peacock; *Bagha* comes from *bagh*, tiger.

## The levels

Each level opens with a story card (a picture of the new place and two or three lines to read aloud), has
five or six puzzles that each tell a small moment, and ends with "*Friend* is coming to the Mela!".
A level opens once four puzzles in the level before have a star, so one tricky puzzle never blocks a child.

| # | Stop | Festival / culture | Puzzles |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Jalebi Junction** (home) | The jalebi shop, chai at the station, getting ready to go | Gappu jigsaw · Who's Coming Along? (shadows) · Pack the Snacks (snap) · Chunnu jigsaw · Jalebi Hunt (maze) · All Aboard! (station jigsaw) |
| 2 | **Punjab: Golden Fields** | Baisakhi harvest, mustard fields, dhol, lassi, makki di roti | Meet Bholu · Baisakhi Snap · Shadows in the Mustard · Train Through the Fields (tracks) · Find Bholu's Dhol (maze) |
| 3 | **Rajasthan: Desert Fair** | Pushkar fair, kathputli puppets, Mandana floor art, ghevar, dal baati | Meet Kesar · Mandana Pattern (rangoli) · Desert Shadows · Desert Snap · Puppet Hunt (maze) |
| 4 | **Gujarat: Kite Festival** | Uttarayan kites, garba, dhokla, fafda with jalebi | Meet Raja · Kite Day Express (tracks) · Farsan Snap · Rooftop Shadows · Kites in the Sky (slide) |
| 5 | **Mumbai: Monsoon City** | Rain, local trains, Ganesh Chaturthi, vada pav, modaks | Meet Chikki · Modak Hunt (maze) · Chaturthi Rangoli · Chowpatty Snack Snap · Monsoon Local (tracks) |
| 6 | **Kerala: Backwaters** | Onam, pookalam flower carpets, snake-boat races, coconut palms | Meet Appu · Backwater Shadows · Onam Pookalam (rangoli) · Snake Boat Race (slide) · Backwater Express (tracks) |
| 7 | **Tamil Nadu: Temple Town** | Pongal, kolam, sugarcane, idli and dosa, temple towers | Meet Mayil · Pongal Kolam (rangoli) · Temple Town Tracks · Peacock Shadows · Tiffin Snap |
| 8 | **Kolkata: City of Joy** | Durga Puja, alpana, Howrah Bridge, yellow taxis, rasgulla, mishti doi | Meet Bagha · Mishti Snap · Shadows by the River · Durga Puja Alpana (rangoli) · Howrah Express (tracks) |
| 9 | **The Hills: Darjeeling & Sikkim** | The toy train, tea gardens, Kanchenjunga, momos | Meet Momo · Toy Train in the Hills (slide) · Picnic Snap · Hill Hunt (maze) · Loops in the Hills (tracks) |
| 10 | **Home for Diwali** | Diyas, lanterns, mithai from everywhere, the Mela | Gappu's Diwali Shop · Diwali Express · Lamplight Shadows · Mithai Snap · Lantern Walk · **The Big Picture** |

Getting harder, level by level: jigsaws grow from 6 to 24 pieces (spinning pieces from level 10), shadows
from 3 friends to 6 with a friend who has no shadow, Snack Snap from 3 pairs (with a peek) to 8, tracks from
4 × 4 to 6 × 6 with decoy pieces, mazes add levers, monkeys and a switch, and rangoli patterns grow from three
plain rings to five rings in two colours.

## Rangoli, the new game

Floor art made for festivals all over India, in many styles. The child sees a finished pattern, picks a
colour pot and taps a ring; every matching petal fills at once, the way a rangoli repeats round and round.
Make it look like the picture to win. The same game appears in five regional styles:

| Style | Where | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Mandana | Rajasthan | White and yellow on red earth |
| Rangoli | Maharashtra, all over India | Bright powder colours |
| Pookalam | Kerala, at Onam | Flower petals: marigold, white, purple, green leaves |
| Kolam | Tamil Nadu | White rice-flour lines with a little colour, on a dark doorstep |
| Alpana | Bengal | White rice paste on red |

Every level's puzzles have their own Pip's fact on the finish screen: a short, true fact about that place,
its food, festival or animal.

## Where it lives in the code

- `app/src/data/levels.ts`: the 10 levels (place, friend, colours, decoration, story card text, finale text)
- `app/src/data/puzzles.ts`: every built-in puzzle, in level order (52 of them)
- `app/src/app/story/[level].tsx`: the story card; `app/src/components/LevelParty.tsx`: a level's finale
- `app/src/data/voice.ts`: the narrator's lines in each stop's language (Punjabi in Punjab, Gujarati in Gujarat…);
  `npm run voice` turns them into `app/assets/voice/` with one female voice (Sarvam AI)
- `app/src/game/rangoli.ts`, `app/src/app/rangoli/[id].tsx`: the Rangoli game
- `app/scripts/art/`: the art kit; `npm run art` draws every friend, place, snack and gift
- `backend/content/ids.py`: the same levels, friends, snacks and pictures, for the server's checks
