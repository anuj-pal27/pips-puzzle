**Long description**

Pip's Puzzles is a bright, gentle puzzle game for young children. Pip the puzzle piece and friends Gappu the goat, Mithu the parrot, Tillu the tortoise and Chunnu the monkey bring six kinds of puzzles:

• Jigsaw puzzles: drag each piece into place to make the picture
• Who's That Shadow?: match each friend to their shadow
• Snack Snap: flip the cards and find the pairs of snacks
• Fix the Tracks: turn the tracks so the jalebi train reaches the stall
• Jalebi Hunt: guide Gappu through town and collect laddoos on the way
• Slide puzzles: slide the tiles to fix the picture

Puzzles go from Easy to Medium to Hard, and each new set unlocks as your child plays. New puzzles arrive automatically, without an app update. Every finished puzzle earns up to 3 stars and a cheer.

Made for little hands:
• Big, friendly buttons and simple drag and tap controls
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat
• Plays offline; new puzzles download when the device is online
• A grown-ups area behind a parent check, for sound, picture hints, privacy and resetting progress

Privacy: the game sends only anonymous play stats (which puzzles are started and finished) to help improve the puzzles. No names, contacts or location. Grown-ups can switch this off in Grown-ups → Settings → Share play stats. Full policy: https://pips-puzzles-api.onrender.com/privacy/

Use **Auto Translate** in the console to add Hindi and other Indian languages.
