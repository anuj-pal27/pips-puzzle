## Description

**Pip's Puzzles** is a bright, gentle puzzle game for young children. Pip the puzzle piece and friends Gappu the goat, Mithu the parrot, Tillu the tortoise and Chunnu the monkey bring you more puzzles than you think, across 6 kinds of games:

- **Jigsaw puzzles**: drag each piece into place to make the picture
- **Who's That Shadow?**: match each friend to their shadow
- **Snack Snap**: flip the cards and find the pairs
- **Fix the Tracks**: turn the tracks so the jalebi train gets to the stall
- **Jalebi Hunt**: guide Gappu through town, collecting laddoos on the way
- **Slide puzzles**: slide the tiles to fix the picture

Puzzles go from **Easy** to **Medium** to **Hard**, and each new set unlocks as your child plays. New puzzles keep arriving without an app update. Every finished puzzle earns up to 3 stars and a cheer.

- No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
- Big, friendly buttons made for little hands
- Plays offline. New puzzles arrive automatically when the phone is online.
- A grown-ups area (behind a parent check) for sound, hints, privacy and resetting progress

### How to install (Android)

1. Download the `.apk` file below on your Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it. If your phone asks, allow your browser to **Install unknown apps**.
3. If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap **More details → Install anyway**.

Works on Android phones and tablets with ARM processors, which is almost every Android device.

To update, download the new `.apk` and install it over the old one. Your child's stars are kept.

### Privacy

Pip's Puzzles sends anonymous play stats: which puzzles are started and finished, and how long they took. This helps make the puzzles better. Each phone gets only a random id, with no names, accounts, location or ads. Grown-ups can switch this off under **Grown-ups → Settings → Privacy**.
